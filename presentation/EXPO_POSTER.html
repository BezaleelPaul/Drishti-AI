🚨 The Clinical Problem

India has 77+ million diabetic patients, yet rural areas suffer from only 1 ophthalmologist per 100,000 citizens.

  • Asymptomatic Blindness: Diabetic Retinopathy causes irreversible capillary loss before vision drops.
  • The AI Failure Mode: Commercial deep learning models force diagnoses even on blurry, ungradable handheld captures—causing catastrophic false triage.
  • Hardware Monopoly: Standard tools require ₹20L tabletop machines and cloud GPUs unreachable by rural PHCs.
⚡ 3-Stage Gated Architecture
Stage 0: Fundus Domain Gate
Rejects non-retinal fixtures (skin, documents, room backgrounds). 100% rejection rate on adversarial red-team corpus.
Stage 1: Model 1 Quality Gate
Multi-scale Laplacian blur, illumination & FOV triage (<180ms). Biological root cause feedback (cataract vs. pupil dilation).
Stage 2: DRDetect Classifier
EfficientNetB0 Ordinal Regression head on 512px. Continuous severity decoding (Grades 0 to 4).
Stage 3: Explainability & Biomarkers
Grad-CAM++ visual heatmaps in <1.2s on CPU. Optic disc, fovea, & CSME macular risk segmentation.

💡 Core Engineering Innovations
  • Zero Diagnostic Leakage: Enforces a strict fail-closed contract. Degraded captures are never assigned a speculative DR grade.
  • 100% Offline Edge Execution: Runs completely without internet or external cloud APIs on entry-level Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 laptops.
  • Real-Time Hindi Audio Guidance: Equips rural ASHA workers with instant spoken coaching on patient positioning and camera focus.
  • Durable SQLCipher Offline Queue: Screenings and encrypted medical records survive phone power-off and sync automatically upon reconnection.
  • MathWorks Telemedicine Model: 100,000-patient Simulink queuing simulation proves 99.1% bandwidth savings across rural district networks.

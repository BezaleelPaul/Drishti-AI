👋 HI SINDURI! READ THIS FIRST (SUPER SIMPLE FIGMA GUIDE)
====================================================================

Don't worry about any complicated code or medical terms!
Your job is super fun and easy: you just need to draw 3 simple phone screens in Figma so our team can show them on the presentation slides.

Everything you need is explained below in 4 easy steps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN FIGMA & START A NEW FILE (Takes 1 minute)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to your browser and open: https://www.figma.com (Login or sign up for free).
2. Click the "+ New design file" button on the top right.
3. On your keyboard, press the letter "F".
4. On the right side menu, click "Phone" and pick "Android Large" (or "iPhone 14").
   -> A blank phone screen will appear on your canvas!
5. Do this 3 times so you have 3 blank phone screens side-by-side.
   -> Name them:
      Screen 1: Patient Check-In
      Screen 2: Camera Warning (Blurry)
      Screen 3: Final Result & SMS

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: WHAT TO PUT ON EACH SCREEN (Takes 20-30 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

📱 SCREEN 1: "Patient Check-In"
   (What the health worker sees when a villager walks into the clinic)
   - At the top: Write "Netra-AI Rural Health Screening"
   - Box 1: Text box saying "Patient Name: Ramesh Kumar (Age: 54)"
   - Box 2: Text box saying "Ayushman Card (ABHA ID): 91-4521-8890-3321"
   - Box 3: Three little buttons for Diabetes: [ < 5 yrs ] [ 5-10 yrs ] [ > 10 yrs ]
   - At the bottom: A big Blue button saying "Start Eye Scan 📸"

📱 SCREEN 2: "Camera Warning (Blurry)"
   (This is the coolest part! It shows our AI warning the worker if photo is bad)
   - Go to the "assets" folder inside this zip file.
   - Drag and drop the picture named "1_blurry_eye_retake.jpg" onto the center of Screen 2.
   - Put a big Yellow / Orange alert box below the picture saying:
     "⚠️ PHOTO TOO BLURRY - PLEASE RETAKE"
     "Tips: Move camera 2 cm closer to patient's eye."
   - A button with a speaker icon: "🔊 Listen in Hindi: 'कैमरा पास लाएं'"
   - A big button at the bottom: "🔄 Retake Photo"

📱 SCREEN 3: "Final Result & SMS"
   (What happens when a good photo is analyzed by the AI)
   - Drag and drop "3_severe_eye_referral.jpg" (or "2_clear_eye_normal.jpg") onto the screen.
   - Put a big RED box saying:
     "🔴 RESULT: SEVERE RETINOPATHY (HIGH RISK)"
     "Action: Urgent Doctor Visit Needed in 30 Days"
   - A box showing: "Patient Mobile: +91 98451 22340"
   - A big Green or Blue button saying: "📲 Send SMS Referral Slip to Patient"

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ONLY 3 COLORS YOU NEED TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
You don't need a huge color palette! Just use these 3 simple colors:
- Blue (for normal buttons): #1A56DB
- Red (for danger / warnings): #DC2626
- Yellow/Orange (for blurry retake alert): #D97706
- Background of screens: Light grey or white (#F8FAFC)

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: HOW TO SEND IT BACK TO BEZALEEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
When you finish drawing the 3 screens:
1. Look at the top right of Figma and click the blue "Share" button.
2. Make sure it says "Anyone with the link can view".
3. Click "Copy link".
4. Paste that link in the team WhatsApp group to Bezaleel!

That's literally all you need to do! You are going to do great! 🎉
If you ever want to see more advanced details or clinical numbers later, check the folder named "extra_details_if_needed".

### 🎯 The Real-World Design Challenge
You are designing for **two completely different users**:

1. **User 1: The Rural ASHA Worker (Field Mobile/Tablet)**
   - **Who she is:** Sunita, 38, community health worker in rural Maharashtra.
   - **Her environment:** High sunlight glare, dusty PHC room, holding an 8-inch Android tablet connected to a handheld camera (Forus 3nethra).
   - **Her pain point:** She is not a doctor and cannot read complex medical jargon. If the photo is blurry, she needs immediate visual guidance in her own language (Hindi/Marathi/etc.) on how to fix it.
   - **What she needs:** Giant touch buttons (minimum 48–56px), high-contrast text (WCAG AAA), clear color-coded statuses (Green = Safe, Red = Urgent Referral), and zero medical confusion.

2. **User 2: The District Tele-Ophthalmologist (Desktop Web Portal)**
   - **Who he is:** Dr. Verma, senior eye specialist at the district civil hospital.
   - **His pain point:** Overburdened with thousands of cases. He has only 30 seconds per patient.
   - **What he needs:** A clean, dark/light clinical console showing side-by-side retinal comparisons (Raw Handheld vs. CLAHE Enhanced vs. Vessel Tree & Grad-CAM Heatmap), with 1-click clinical sign-off and laser photocoagulation referral.

# 🖼️ 06. Presentation Slide Mockups & Pitch Script
## How to Turn Your Figma Designs into Award-Winning Slides

Hackathon winners never just paste flat rectangular screenshots onto white PowerPoint slides. They present their designs in **clean 3D device mockups** (tablets and laptops).

---

### 1. Free Figma Plugins to Generate Instant Mockups
Inside Figma, right-click $\to$ **Plugins** $\to$ search for any of these 100% free plugins:
1. **Mockup** (by Cleanmock / Alexander):
   - Select your ASHA mobile screen $\to$ Choose "Clay Tablet" or "Android Phone" $\to$ Done!
2. **Clay Device Mockups** (Minimalist, modern, distraction-free):
   - Perfect for medical healthtech apps.
3. **Angle 3** (Perspective screen mockups).

---

### 2. The 2 Slides You Should Build for the Team Deck

#### Slide A: "Designed for Rural India: The ASHA Field Experience"
* **Visual:**
  * Left: Clay tablet mockup of Screen 2 (Retinal Viewfinder HUD).
  * Right: Clay phone mockup of Screen 3 (Hindi Audio Blur Guidance + SMS slip).
* **3 Bullet Points:**
  1. **Zero Jargon:** Color-coded triage (Green/Red) designed for non-physician ASHA workers.
  2. **Vernacular Alignment Guide:** Hindi/local language voice prompts solve the #1 rural failure: motion blur.
  3. **1-Click Connectivity:** Generates offline SMS referral slips that patients can take to district hospitals.

#### Slide B: "Specialist Tele-Review Station"
* **Visual:**
  * MacBook / Desktop monitor mockup of the 3-panel synchronized retinal viewer.
* **3 Bullet Points:**
  1. **Explainable AI:** Grad-CAM heatmap rendered in 0.10s pinpointing microaneurysms.
  2. **MathWorks Compliance:** Live vessel density extraction and foveal CSME edema risk.
  3. **ABDM EMR Integration:** 1-click HL7 FHIR export directly into Ayushman Bharat health records.

### 3. Sinduri's Exact 45-Second Pitch Script
When the team is presenting on Google Meet / Stage, this is Sinduri's cue:

> *"Hello judges, I'm Sinduri, the UI/UX lead.  
> We knew that the world's most advanced AI is useless if a rural health worker cannot use it in a dusty village health center.  
> That is why we designed our interface from the ground up for ASHA workers. It features high-contrast touch targets, an intelligent camera alignment viewfinder, and vernacular voice instructions that tell the worker in Hindi if the camera is out of focus.  
> When high-risk retinopathy is detected, it instantly dispatches a simple SMS referral slip to the patient's phone and pushes an ABDM-compliant clinical dossier to Dr. Verma at the district hospital.  
> Now, my teammate Bezaleel will demonstrate the live AI engine running in real time."*
